## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design evokes the feeling of the subject rather than depicting it. Use one restrained, slightly imperfect monoline and extreme negative space. The subject may be very small, shifted, partial or only a fragment of its contour. At most a few supporting lines — a horizon, a ripple, a ridge — and only if they serve the mood. Reject a complete illustration outline, a filled scene, extra objects and a line that looks mechanically even.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
